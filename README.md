- Bell peppers: wash, slice, toss in avocado oil, and roast at 375 F on metal sheet pan (no parchment) until soft
- Green beans: toss in avocado oil and 1/2 teaspoon black pepper, roast at 375 F on metal sheet pan (no parchment) until soft
- Asparagus: wash, peel, boil 6–8 minutes or until easily piercable with fork, rinse under cold water
- Farro: cook 1 cup according to instructions on bag
