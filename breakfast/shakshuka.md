# Shakshuka
Adapted from Love & Lemons and other sources

## Ingredients
- 1 cup chopped yellow onion
- 1 red bell pepper, stemmed, seeded, and diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- 1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons harissa paste // omit
- 1 cup fresh spinach, chopped
- 8 large eggs
- 1/3 cup crumbled feta cheese
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley leaves
- 1 avocado, sliced
- Micro greens, for garnish, optional
- Toasted bread, for serving 

## Instructions
1. (Optional: If using oven to cook eggs, preheat to 375. Can either cook on stovetop the whole time or start on stovetop and move to oven at the end - whichever is easier based on current kitchen needs.) 
2. Heat olive oil in large stainless steel skillet over medium heat. Add onion and pepper, then cook for 8–10 minutes or until 3. softened. Add garlic and spices, then stir for a minute or until fragrant. 
4. Add tomatoes, harissa paste, and spinach. Simmer over low heat and stir often until sauce has thickened, about 15 minutes. 
5. Use a large spoon to make 8 wells in the sauce. Crack 1 egg into each well. (If switching to oven, put skillet in oven.) 
6. Cook 4–8 minutes or until eggs are set. Yolks should be light orange but not runny. 
7. Serve in the skillet. Top with feta, parsley, avocado, and micro greens. Serve with bread.
