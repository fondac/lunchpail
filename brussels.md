# Brussels sprouts

1. Wash brussels, trim, and cut in half. Place in steamer basket. 
2. Boil 1 inch water in saucepan.
3. Put steamer basket in saucepan. Steam for 7–10 minutes or until fork-tender.
4. Set half of brussels aside.
5. Place remaining half on metal sheet pan and toss in avocado oil.
6. Broil for a few minutes or until outside is crispy.
