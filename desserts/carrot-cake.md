# Carrot cake

## Ingredients
Cake
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1.5 cups grated carrots (you can grate the carrots in a food processor fitted with a shredding blade)
- 1/2 cup coarsely chopped walnuts 
- 1/4 cup moist, plump raisins (can soak in hot water)
- 1 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup canola, safflower, or other neutral oil
- 2 large eggs 

Frosting
- 6 ounces cream cheese, room temperature 
- 3/4 stick unsalted butter, room temperature
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Generous pinch of kosher salt
- If desired: chopped walnuts, lightly toasted

## Instructions
1. Set out cream cheese and butter to soften to room temperature.
2. Preheat the oven to 325 F degrees. Place the racks in the top middle and bottom middle. Butter and flour a 9-inch round cake pan, then tap out the excess flour.
3. In a medium-sized bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, and salt.
4. In another medium-sized bowl, stir together the carrots, nuts, and raisins.
5. In a large bowl, using a standing or handheld mixer, beat the sugar and oil together until smooth. Add the eggs one at a time and continue beating.
6. Gently stir the flour mixture into the sugar mixture until just incorporated, then stir in the carrot mixture until just incorporated (don't over-mix).
7. Pour the batter into the baking pan. Bake for 40–50 minutes, starting the pan on the higher rack and moving it to the lower rack about halfway through baking. The cake is done when a knife or fork inserted into the center comes out clean and edges just start to come away from the pan. 
8. Transfer the pan to a cooling rack for 5–10 minutes. Turn the cake out onto the rack and cool to room temperature. If you need to store the cake overnight, wrap it airtight after cooling.
9. Make the frosting. In a large bowl, using a standing or handheld mixer, beat the cream cheese and butter together until smooth. Gradually beat in the powdered sugar in 3–4 batches until smooth. Beat in the vanilla and salt until fully incorporated.
10. Place the cake right-side up on a cake plate. Frost the cake on the top and, if desired, the sides. If nuts are desired on top of the frosting, add them now while the frosting is soft. You can refrigerate the cake for 15 minutes if you prefer a firmer frosting.
