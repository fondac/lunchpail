# Aloo (potato) curry

## Ingredients
- 1 pound frozen peas
- 2 tablespoons avocado oil
- 2 shallots, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 1/2-inch piece ginger, peeled and minced
- 1.25 tsp garam masala
- 2 pounds gold potatoes, peeled and cut into 3/4-inch pieces 
- 6 cups leafy greens such as mustard or dandelion, destemmed and roughly chopped
- 10 curry leaves, minced // defrosted from round container in freezer
- 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek)
- 1 (13-ounce) can unsweetened coconut milk, well shaken
- 3–4 cups water, if needed
- 1 lime, juiced
- For serving, optional: lime slices, handful of cilantro leaves

## Instructions
1. Pull peas out of freezer to defrost them. 
2. Heat oil in Dutch oven over medium-low heat. Add shallot and sauté until transparent.
3. Stir in garlic and ginger, then sauté until aromatic. Stir in garam masala and sauté until aromatic.
4. Stir in potatoes and greens. Sauté for 2–3 minutes. Stir in curry leaves, fenugreek, and coconut milk. If potatoes aren't submerged in liquid, add enough water to cover them.
5. Bring to boil then simmer over medium-low heat until potatoes are tender, maybe 15–17 min.
6. Stir in peas and cook a few minutes until peas are no longer cold. Add lime juice and cook 2 more minutes to meld flavors.
7. Serve with lime slices and cilantro.
