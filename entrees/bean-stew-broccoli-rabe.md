# White bean stew with broccoli rabe
Based on NYT

## Ingredients
- 1 large bunch (or 2 small bunches) broccoli rabe or kale, thick stems separated from the leaves
- 1/4 cup avocado oil
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 medium red onion, thinly sliced
- Ground black black pepper
- 3 tablespoons harissa or tomato paste // use tomato paste
- 1 15-oz can large white beans, such as cannellini, butter or great Northern, drained and rinsed
- 4 cups vegetable or chicken broth
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 2 ounces feta or other salty cheese, such as queso fresco or pecorino, crumbled - for topping 
- 1 cup parsley or cilantro, leaves and tender stems - for topping 
- Bakery bread or rolls - for serving 

## Instructions
1. Tear broccoli rabe or kale leaves into bite-size pieces and set aside. Chop the stems into about 1/4-inch pieces; set aside.
2. Heat oil in Dutch oven over medium heat. Add garlic, onion, vegetable stems, and a few shakes of ground black pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until onion is nearly translucent.
3. Add harissa and stir to coat in the oil. Cook until sauce is a fiery brick color about 2 minutes.
4. Add beans. Using a wooden spoon or spatula, crush a few beans to release their creamy interior.
5. Add broth. Bring to a simmer and cook until stew has thickened, 15 to 20 minutes.
6. Add vegetable leaves and lemon juice. Stir to wilt the greens. 
7. Serve with cheese, herbs, and bread
