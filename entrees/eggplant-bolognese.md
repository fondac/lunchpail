# Eggplant bolognese
Based on [NYT](https://web.archive.org/web/20251113091034/https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1025692-eggplant-bolognese)

## Ingredients
- Salt and pepper
- 6 tablespoons avocado oil
- 1 pound Italian eggplant, peeled and chopped into 1/4-inch pieces (4 packed cups)
- 6 ounces cremini (or white button) mushrooms, finely chopped (2 cups)
- 1/2 cup finely chopped white onion
- 1/2 cup finely chopped carrot
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 3 cups broth
- 1 cup canned whole tomatoes, crushed with your hands 
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 basil sprig, plus chopped basil for garnishing
- 1 pound rigatoni, fusilli or other short pasta
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- Freshly grated Parmesan, for serving

## Instructions
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil over high. Drop pasta into the boiling water and cook according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of the pasta water before draining.
2. In a large Dutch oven, heat 2 tablespoons of oil over medium heat. Add half of the eggplant, and season lightly with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and lightly golden, about 5 minutes. Transfer eggplant to a plate. Repeat with 2 more tablespoons of oil and the remaining eggplant.
3. Add the remaining 2 tablespoons oil and the mushrooms to the pot. Season lightly with salt and pepper and cook, stirring occasionally to scrape up any browned bits, until golden, about 5 minutes.
4. Add onion and carrot. Cook, stirring occasionally, until onion is softened, about 5 minutes. Adjust heat to low, then add garlic and tomato paste. Stir until caramelized, about 2 minutes.
5. Stir in broth, tomatoes, oregano, basil sprig, and eggplant. Bring to a boil over medium-high. Cover, reduce heat to medium-low and cook, stirring occasionally, until eggplant is very tender, about 15 minutes. Uncover and cook, stirring and mashing half of the eggplant, until sauce is thickened, 2 minutes.
6. Add pasta, butter, and 1/2 cup of the pasta water to the eggplant mixture. Cook, stirring vigorously, until pasta is coated and mixture is saucy, about 2–3 minutes. 
7. Serve with chopped basil and cheese.
