# Eggplant parmesan

## Ingredients
- 2 large eggplants, peeled and cut into 1/2-inch-thick rounds
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- 1/4 cup milk
- 3.5 cups panko breadcrumbs, divided
- 1.25 cups grated Parmesan cheese, divided
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 2 tablespoons fresh thyme leaves
- [omit] 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- Ground black pepper
- Cooking olive oil, for drizzling
- 3 15-oz cans tomato sauce
- Garlic powder, onion powder, dried basil, and oregano to sprinkle on top of each layer of tomato sauce 
- 2 large fresh mozzarella balls, ripped into small
- 1/3 cup fresh basil leaves

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and pull out 2 baking sheet pans.
2. In a medium-sized shallow dish, whisk the eggs and milk.
3. In another medium-sized shallow dish, combine 2 cups panko breadcrumbs, 1 cup Parmesan cheese, oregano, thyme, red pepper flakes, salt, and several shakes of pepper.
4. Dip the eggplant slices into the egg mixture and then into the panko mixture. Place onto the baking sheets and drizzle generously with olive oil.
5. Bake for about 35 minutes or until very tender, flipping slices halfway through.
6. Remove eggplant and change oven temp to 400 F.
7. In an 8x12 or 9x13-inch baking dish, spread a layer of tomato sauce and spices (see list above). Layer half the eggplant, another layer of tomato sauce and spices, and half the mozzarella.
8. Repeat with the remaining eggplant, a layer of sauce and spices, and the remaining mozzarella. Sprinkle with the remaining 1.5 cups panko breadcrumbs and 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese, and drizzle with olive oil.
9. Bake for 20 minutes or until the cheese is nicely melted. 
10. Remove from the oven and top with fresh basil.
