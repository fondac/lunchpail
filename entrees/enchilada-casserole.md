# Enchilada casserole
Based on [The Modern Proper](https://themodernproper.com/enchilada-casserole) and [Cookie and Kate](https://cookieandkate.com/classic-pico-de-gallo-recipe/)

## Ingredients

### Pico de gallo
- 1 small white onion, finely chopped
- 2 limes, juiced
- 3/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 1.5 pounds ripe red tomatoes (about 8 small or 4 large), chopped
- 0.5 cup finely chopped fresh cilantro (about 1 bunch)

### Sauce
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 2 tablespoons avocado oil
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1.5 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 3 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2 cups chicken broth

### Casserole
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 1 medium red bell pepper, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 (7-ounce) can diced green chiles
- 1 (15-ounce) can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 cups corn, frozen
- 12 (6-inch) corn tortillas
- 1.5 cups shredded pepper jack cheese
- 1.5 cups shredded cheddar cheese

## Instructions  

### Pico de gallo 
1. In a medium serving bowl, combine the chopped onion, lime juice, and salt. Let it marinate for about 5 minutes.
2. Chop tomatoes and cilantro. Add to the bowl and stir to combine. Taste, and add more salt if the flavors don’t quite sing.
3. For the best flavor, let the mixture marinate for 15 minutes or several hours in the refrigerator. 

### Sauce
1. Preheat oven to 350 F.
2. Place the oil and flour in a medium saucepan. Cook, whisking constantly, over medium heat until smooth, about 6 minutes.
3. Once bubbling, whisk in the chili powder, garlic powder, onion powder, cumin, oregano, and tomato paste.
4. Once combined, slowly whisk in chicken stock and salt and bring to a simmer.
5. Once simmering, reduce the heat to low and cook, stirring occasionally, for about 5 minutes, or until slightly thickened. 

### Casserole
1. Heat avocado oil a large stainless steel skillet over medium heat. Add the ground beef and black pepper. Cook, breaking it up with a wooden spoon, until browned, about 8-10 minutes.
2. Add the onion and bell pepper and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
3. Stir in the garlic and cook until fragrant, 1 minute more.
4. Stir in the green chiles, beans, corn and half of the enchilada sauce. Cook until warmed through, about 5 more minutes. Remove from heat.  

### Assembly
1. Dip half of the tortillas in the enchilada sauce, then arrange in a single layer on the bottom of a large (9 x 13 inch) baking dish.
2. Top with half of the beef filling and half of the pepper jack and cheddar.
3. Dip the remaining tortillas in the remaining enchilada sauce and arrange in a single layer on top of the cheese.
4. Top with the remaining filling and remaining cheese.
5. Bake for 25–35 minutes or until the cheese is bubbly and golden. 
6. Serve with pico de gallo.
