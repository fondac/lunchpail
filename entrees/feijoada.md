# Feijoada (Brazilian bean stew)
Not including smoked/salted meats. Based on [Daring Gourmet](https://www.daringgourmet.com/feijoada/#recipe), [Dad Cooks Dinner](https://www.dadcooksdinner.com/pressure-cooker-feijoada-brazilian-black-bean-and-meat-stew/)

## Ingredients
- 3 Tbsp cooking oil (I use avocado oil as it has a high smoke point but you can also use canola)
- 1 pound pork shoulder // can cut into 1-inch cubes before cooking if prefer not to shred at end
- 1 pound boneless beef short ribs // can cut into 1-inch cubes before cooking if prefer not to shred at end
- 2 large onions, diced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 pound dried black beans, picked through and rinsed
- 4 bay leaves
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 quarts beef broth
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda

Serve with: 
- Rice
- Fresh orange slices
- Farofa: toasted cassava (manioc) flour
- Garlicky sautéed collard greens

## Instructions
1. Set the Instant Pot to the sauté function for 30 minutes. Heat the oil and cook the pork shoulder until light brown on each side. Remove pork shoudler and set aside.
2. Cook short ribs until light brown on each side. Remove ribs and set aside.
3. Add the onions and cook the onions until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for another minute. Scrape up any browned bits. Cancel sauté function.
4. Return the pork and beef to the pot. Add the beans, bay leaves, black pepper, and salt.
5. Pour in beef broth until meat is just barely covered. Add the baking soda and mix well. 
6. Cook on high pressure for 50 minutes. Allow for natural pressure release.
7. Open lid carefully and make sure beans are soft.
8. If feijoada is too liquidy, move pot to stovetop over medium heat. Cook to allow some of liquid to evaporate, maybe 20 minutes.
9. While liquid is evaporating, discard bay leaves and shred meat.
10. Serve with side dishes.
