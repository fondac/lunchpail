# Feijoada (Brazilian bean stew)
Not including smoked/salted meats. Based on [Daring Gourmet](https://www.daringgourmet.com/feijoada/#recipe), [Dad Cooks Dinner](https://www.dadcooksdinner.com/pressure-cooker-feijoada-brazilian-black-bean-and-meat-stew/)

## Ingredients
- 3 T avocado oil
- 1 pound pork shoulder, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1 pound boneless beef short ribs, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 2 large onions, diced
- 6 cloves garlic, sliced thinly
- 1 pound dried black beans, picked through and rinsed
- 4 bay leaves
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 quarts chicken broth (will probably not need all of it)
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda

Serve with: 
- Rice
- Fresh orange slices
- Garlicky sautéed collard greens (sliced very thinly)

## Instructions
1. (optional) Set the Instant Pot to the sauté function for 30 minutes. Heat the oil and cook the pork shoulder until light brown on each side. Remove pork shoudler and set aside.
2. (optional) Cook short ribs until light brown on each side. Remove ribs and set aside.
3. (optional) Add the onions and cook the onions until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for another minute. Scrape up any browned bits. Cancel sauté function.
4. Return the pork and beef to the pot. Add the beans, bay leaves, black pepper, and salt.
5. Pour in broth until meat is just barely covered. Add the baking soda and mix well. 
6. Cook on high pressure for 50 minutes. Allow for natural pressure release.
7. If feijoada is too liquidy, move pot to stovetop over medium heat. Cook to allow some of liquid to evaporate, maybe 20 minutes.
8. While liquid is evaporating, discard bay leaves.
9. Serve with side dishes.
