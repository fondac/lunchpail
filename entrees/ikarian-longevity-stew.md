# Ikarian longevity stew

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup cooking olive oil
- 1 fennel, finely chopped with tender fronds reserved
- 3 large shallots or 1 large red onion, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 15oz. can of black-eyed peas, drained & rinsed
- 2 large Roma tomatoes, finely chopped
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 bunch fresh dill
- For serving: extra-virgin olive oil

## Instructions
1. In a medium pot, heat the oil over medium heat. Add the fennel, shallot or onion, and garlic. Cook until soft. 
2. Add salt, pepper, tomato paste, and water. Mix well. Stir in the black-eyed peas, tomatoes, and bay leaves. 
3. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 15 mimutes. 
4. Stir in dill and fennel fronds. Simmer for 15 minutes or until black-eyed peas are tender. 
5. Turn off heat and discard bay leaves. Serve drizzled with extra-virgin olive oil.
