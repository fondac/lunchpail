# Koshari

Make sauces ahead of time so flavors meld. Can make onions ahead of time then reheat in oven at 350 F for 5–7 min. 

## Ingredients
Shallots
- 1 cup neutral frying oil, such as canola
- 10 medium shallots, thinly sliced

Salsa 
- 4 tablespoons shallot-infused oil (recipe above)
- 4 medium cloves garlic, minced 
- 4 tablespoons distilled white vinegar
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2 teaspoon ground cumin // next time try baharat from gdoc
- 2 teaspoon ground coriander
- 2 15-oz cans plain tomato sauce

Koshari 
- 1/2 cup onion-infused oil (recipe above)
- 1/2 cup white basmati rice, triple rinsed then drained and soaked in cold water for 30 minutes (this prep is first step in recipe)
- 1/2 cup black lentils, soaked in hot water for 1 hour (this prep is first step in recipe)
- 1 cup elbow pasta
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin 
- 2 teaspoons ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper

Serving
- 1 14.5 oz can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 3 limes, sliced
- Red chili pepper flakes
- 1 cucumber, peeled and cut into spears

## Instructions

**Soak rice and lentils**
1. Soak lentils in hot water.
2. Rinse rice 3 times, then drain and soak in cold water. 

**Fry the shallots**
1. In a small stainless steel skillet, combine oil and shallots over medium heat. Cook, stirring often, until shallots are light gold and crisp, about 20–30 minutes.
2. Strain through a fine-mesh strainer into a heatproof bowl. Transfer shallots to a paper-towel lined plate to drain. Set shallot-infused oil aside to cool.
3. Store the fried shallots in an airtight container until you are ready to use. Store the shallot-infused oil in a clean jar once cooled.

**Make the salsa**
1. In a saucepan, heat 4 tablespoons of shallot-infused oil over medium-low heat until shimmering. Add garlic and cook, stirring constantly, until garlic is fragrant and light gold, 1 to 2 minutes.
2. Whisk in vinegar, tomato paste, cumin, and coriander until smooth.
3. Add tomato sauce. Simmer on low until slightly thickened, about 10 minutes. The sauce should have the consistency of a marinara.
4. Remove from heat and set aside uncovered.

**Assemble the koshari**
1. Cook the lentils. Boil 1.5 cups hot water. Add with lentils to saucepan. Simmer on medium-low until lentils are soft, about 20 minutes.
2. Boil 4 cups of water and set aside. In a Dutch oven, heat 1/4 cup shallot oil over medium-low heat until shimmering. Add rice and elbow pasta and stir until evenly coated in the oil, 1 to 2 minutes. Stir in the black lentils. Pour in the freshly boiled water; there should be just enough to cover the ingredients.
3. Add cumin, coriander, salt, and black pepper. Bring to a boil over high heat and cook until all the water has been absorbed, about 10 minutes. Stir more frequently as the liquid goes down to stir to prevent sticking (lower heat and drizzle in avocado oil if necessary).
4. Reduce heat to medium, cover with lid, and cook about 3 minutes. Let rest off heat for 1 minute, then uncover the pot. Fluff with a fork.
5. Serve topped with salsa, then chickpeas, then onions. Offer lime, red pepper flakes, and cucumber on the side.
