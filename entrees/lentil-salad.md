# Lentil salad
Based on [Family Style Food](https://familystylefood.com/black-lentil-salad-2/#wprm-recipe-container-15482)

## Ingredients
Sauce
- 1 cup each Italian parsley and cilantro leaves, loosely packed (or 2 cups of either herb)
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup (125 ml) extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar

Lentils
- 1 cup black or French-style lentils, picked through
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 red onion, very thinly sliced 
- 2 Persian cucumbers, juicy seeds removed and diced into small cubes // if not available, substitute 1 English cucumber
- 1/2 cup fresh mint leaves, chopped // if not available, substitute 1 teaspoon dried mint, first rubbed between fingers to finely crush and release oils
- 1/2 cup (125 g) crumbled feta or goat cheese

## Instructions
1. Combine all the ingredients for the sauce in a food processor until very smooth.
2. Cook the lentils. Bring 4 cups water to a boil with the salt. Add the lentils and cook until soft, maybe 25–30 minutes. Drain well and allow to cool to room temperature.
3. Sauté the red onions until soft and translucent. // When only people who like raw onion, skip this step
4. Put the onion, cucumbers, and lentils in a serving bowl. Add 1/3 cup of the green sauce and toss gently.
5. Sprinkle the salad with the mint and crumbled cheese. Toss again.
6. Serve the lentils with additional sauce spooned over.
