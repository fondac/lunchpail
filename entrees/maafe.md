# Maafe
Based on [NYTimes](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1017950-chicken-mafe) and [Gypsy Plate](https://gypsyplate.com/maafe/#recipe-card)

## Ingredients
- 2 pounds boneless chicken (can also use beef)
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- 3 tbsp avocado oil
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 12 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 1/2-inch pieces of ginger, peeled
- 1 1/2 tablespoons fish sauce
- 6 ounces tomato paste (or blended sun-dried tomatoes, if available)
- 3 cups water
- 1 eggplant, cut into small cubes
- 3 carrots, peeled and cut into small half moons
- 1 sweet potato, cut into small cubes
- 2 small yellow potatoes, cut into small cubes
- 1/2 cup creamy unsweetened peanut butter
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- [omit] 1 Scotch Bonnet chile, sliced
- For serving: rice, cilantro

## Instructions
1. Pat chicken dry and cut into chunks. Toss with paprika, salt, and black pepper in large bowl. Set aside. 
2. In a large Dutch oven, heat oil over medium heat. Add onion and cook until soft. Add garlic and garlic, then cook until onion is nearly translucent.
3. Stir in fish sauce and tomato paste. Cook and stir occasionally until paste darkens. Add water and scrape up any browned bits.
4. Add and combine the chicken, eggplant, carrots, sweet potato, and potatoes. Boil and turn heat down to a simmer.
5. In a small bowl, combine a cup of the cooking liquid with the peanut butter until smooth. Pour mixture into pot. Cook for 30 minutes. 
6. Add the bell pepper and cook for another 15–30 minutes until chicken is tender and cooked through.
