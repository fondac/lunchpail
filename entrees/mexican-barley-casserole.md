# Mexican-inspired barley casserole
Based on [Weary Chef](https://fondac.github.io/lunchpail/entrees/enchilada-casserole.html), HT to Dianna

## Ingredients
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 3/4 cup barley
- 1.5 pounds ripe red tomatoes (about 8 small or 4 large), chopped
- 3/4 teaspoon fine salt
- 2 tablespoons avocado oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 green bell pepper, sliced
- 1 pound ground turkey
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup shredded Mexican blend cheese
- 4 green onions, sliced
- Lime slices for serving

## Instructions
1. Bring chicken broth, cumin, and oregano to a low boil in a medium saucepan. Stir in barley, cover, and reduce heat to low. Cook 45 minutes or until liquid is absorbed. Meanwhile, toss the tomatoes with salt and set aside in a strainer. 
2. About 20 minutes before your barley is ready, preheat oven to 400 degrees F.
3. Heat a large stainless steel skillet over medium heat and add oil. Add onion and bell pepper strips. Sauté until vegetables are tender. Move to a bowl.
4. In the same skillet, add the meat and garlic powder. Crumble the meat as it cooks and sauté until cooked through.
5. In a 13 x 9" glass baking dish, spread the barley. Layer the peppers and onion over the barley, then the meat, tomatoes, and cheese. Sprinkle green onions on top. 
6. Bake for approximately 15-20 minutes until cheese is melted.
7. Serve with lime slices.
