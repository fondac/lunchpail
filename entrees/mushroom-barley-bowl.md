# Mushroom barley bowl
[Lone Recipes](https://lonerecipes.com/recipes/mushroombarleybowl.html) via Dianna

## Ingredients
- 1 cup pearl barley
- 3 cups chicken broth
- 1/3 cup pine nuts
- 3 tablespoons avocado oil
- 10 oz mixed mushrooms, sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 bunch kale, ribs and leaves separated, both chopped
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

## Instructions
1. In a medium saucepan, bring the broth to a boil. Add the barley, cover, and simmer over low heat for 35-40 minutes until the barley is tender and has absorbed most of the liquid. If there's excess liquid, drain it. Set the cooked barley aside.
2. While the barley cooks, toast the pine nuts in a dry skillet over medium heat for 3-4 minutes, stirring frequently until they're golden and fragrant. Remove from heat immediately and set aside to prevent burning.
3. In a large skillet, heat the oil over medium-high heat. Add the sliced mushrooms and kale ribs. Cook without stirring for 2–3 minutes to develop a golden crust. Then stir and continue cooking for another 6–7 minutes until mushrooms are tender and kale ribs have softened. 
4. Add the minced garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Stir in the kale leaves, salt, and pepper. Cook for 2-3 minutes until the kale is wilted but still vibrant green.
6. Add barley and cook for 2 minutes to meld flavors. 
7. Serve topped with the toasted pine nuts.
