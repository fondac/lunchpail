# Pastallini
Based on Milk Street cannellini beans and [Smitten Kitchen](https://smittenkitchen.com/2013/01/pasta-and-white-beans-with-garlic-rosemary-oil/)/Sara Jenkins via Bon Appetit

## Ingredients
- 1 yellow onion, cut into big chunks
- 1 carrot, cut into big chunks
- 1 celery stalk, cut into big chunks
- 6 garlic cloves
- 1/2 cup cooking olive oil, divided
- 2.5 tablespoons dried parsley
- 1/2 tablespoon dried rosemary
- 1 tablespoon fennel seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3 tablespoons tomato paste
- 15-ounce can of cannellini beans, rinsed (if using cooked beans, reserve cooking liquid)
- 14.5 oz can diced tomatoes, including packing juices
- 1 pound short pasta // use both bags
- To serve: grated Parmigiano Reggiano, chopped fresh parsley

## Instructions
1. Pulse onion, carrot, celery, and garlic in a food processor until finely chopped.
2. Heat 1/4 cup cooking olive oil in a large, heavy pot over medium heat. Add vegetable mixture, parsley, rosemary, fennel seeds, and salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until vegetables take on a bit of color, about 10 minutes.
3. Rinse food processor and set aside to use again later. 
4. Add tomato paste and cook it into the vegetables for another minute. Add 1 cup water (or bean cooking liquid) and scrape up any bits stuck to the pot. Let simmer until liquid has almost disappeared, about 5 to 8 minutes.
6. Add beans, tomatoes, and 1/2 more cup of water (or bean cooking liquid) to the pot. Simmer until the flavors meld, about another 15 minutes.
7. Meanwhile, cook pasta until al dente. Reserve 1 1/2 cups cooking water from your drained pasta.
8. Transfer one cup of the bean mixture to food processor and purée it until smooth, then stir it back into the pot. Add drained pasta and 1/2 cup cooking liquid to bean sauce and cook until the sauce coats the pasta, about 1–2 more minutes. Add more pasta cooking liquid as needed.
9. Serve with cheese and parsley to taste.
