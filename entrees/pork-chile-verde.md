# Pork chile verde
Based on [NYT](https://web.archive.org/web/20250210010036/https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1025775-pork-chile-verde)

## Ingredients
- 3 garlic cloves, minced (for chard)
- 1 bunch chard, cut into bite-size pieces
- 1 pound tomatillos, husks removed and insides washed
- 4 garlic cloves, peeled
- 3 tablespoons avocado oil
- 1 loosely packed cup cilantro leaves and tender stems, plus chopped leaves for garnishing
- 3 pounds pork loin, cut into bite-size strips about 1/4 x 2 inches
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 2 cups chicken broth, on reserve
- Chopped cilantro for serving

## Instructions
1. In a stainless steel skillet, sauté minced garlic and chard until chard is softened. 
2. Heat broiler on high and set top oven rack 6 inches from the heat. On a large rimmed sheet pan, combine tomatillos, garlic cloves, and 1 tablespoon of the oil. Toss to evenly coat.
3. Broil the mixture about 5–7 minutes, tossing halfway through. Skins should be softened but not charred.
4. Scrape everything from the pan into a food processor. Add the cilantro and purée until smooth.
5. In a large Dutch oven, heat the remaining 2 tablespoons oil over medium. Season pork with salt and pepper and add half to the pot. Cook, turning occasionally, until golden all over. Transfer to a plate and repeat with the remaining pork.
6. Add the onion to the pot and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 7–10 minutes. Add cumin and oregano, and stir until fragrant, about 1 minute.
7. Add back the pork with any accumulated juices, then the chard and tomatillo purée.
8. Add chicken broth as needed to reach a thick stew-like consistency.
9. Serve over rice and topped with cilantro.
