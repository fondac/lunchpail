# Quinoa bowls

## Ingredients
- 2 bags cauliflower, cut into bite-size pieces
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 bunch radishes, sliced into half moons
- 1 block firm tofu, cut into bite-size cubes
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 3+ tablespoons avocado oil
- 2 cups frozen edamame, thawed 
- 1.5 cups quinoa

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375 F. 
2. Mix all but edamame quinoa in large bowl. Toss to combine and evenly coat seasonings/oil on other ingredients.
3. Bake for 20–45 (?) minutes until cauliflower is tender.
4. Meanwhile, set out edamame to thaw and cook quinoa according to instructions on bag. Store baked ingredients, edamame, and quinoa separately.
