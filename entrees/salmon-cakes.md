# Salmon cakes with tomato sauce

## Salmon cakes

### Ingredients
- 1/3 cup quinoa
- 8 oz frozen salmon filets, thawed and baked with avocado oil at 375 F until just cooked
- salt and pepper
- avocado oil 
- 1 red onion or 3 shallots, finely chopped 
- 3/4 cup plain yogurt
- 2 eggs, lightly beaten 
- 1 tsp dried dill
- on reserve: Panko breadcrumbs, more yogurt
- for serving: creme fraiche or sauce of yogurt, lemon juice, sweet paprika 

### Instructions
1. Cook quinoa according to product instructions.
2. Bake salmon with oil and light salt/pepper.
3. Place salmon in large bowl and break into flakes with 2 forks.
4. Add onion/shallots, yogurt, eggs, and dill. If too soft to form patties, add Panko breadcrumbs. If too firm, add yogurt. 
5. Form small patties, each with a diameter about 4 finger widths. 
6. Broil 5 min on each side or until they are golden but not brown.
7. Serve with creme fraiche. 


## Jaew tomato (sauce)

### Ingredients
- Juice from 1 lime
- 2 roma tomatoes, cut in half lengthways
- 5 cloves of garlic, peeled
- 6 small shallots, peeled
- 1 tablespoon of fish sauce
- 1/4 cup of green onion, chopped
- 1/4 cup of cilantro, chopped

### Instructions
1. Broil tomatoes, garlic, and shallots until slightly charred. Let cool.
2. Use a food processor on pulse or a mortar and pestle to mash the tomatoes, garlic, and shallots.
3. Mix in the rest of the ingredients.
