# Shrimp and snow pea stir fry

## Ingredients
Shrimp marinade 
- 10 oz shrimp
- 0.50 tsp salt
- [omit] 0.25 tsp white pepper 
- 1 tbsp cornstarch 

Main ingredients
- 10 oz boiling water
- 4 oz celery, 1-inch strips
- 1 carrot, cut into diamonds
- 1 pack seafood mushroom
- 10 oz snow peas
- 1 tbsp avocado oil
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 thin slices ginger 
- 2 stalk green onion
- 1 dash cooking wine
- 1 dash sesame oil

Sauce 
- 0.50 tsp salt
- [omit] 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp cooking wine
- [omit] 0.25 tsp white pepper  
- 1 tsp fish sauce
- 1.5 tbsp water

## Instructions
1. Marinate shrimp in small bowl to keep them tender. 
2. Prep veggies.
- Rinse snow peas or soak them in water 5–10 min.
- Cut carrots into diamonds. First cut at an angle into 1/2-inch pieces. Then lay them with a cut side down and slice them into 1/8-inch thick slices. 
3. Prep aromatics. 
- Cut green onion at an angle into inch-long pieces
- Smash garlic with flat side of knife, then peel and mince. 
- Cut ends off mushrooms. 
- Mix sauce ingredients in small bowl. 
4. Blanch vegetables
- Pour 10 oz boiling water into wok over medium heat. Add celery, carrot, and mushrooms (if using frozen mushrooms, add them after the snowpeas). Cook until softened, then add snow peas. 
- Cover and cook until veggies soft (not al dente). Set aside, including liquid. 
5. Stir-fry shrimp
- Wipe out wok and heat oil on medium until shimmering. 
- Add ginger and garlic, then cook until fragrant. Add green onion until softened. Add shrimp, 1 dash cooking wine, and sauce (give it a quick mix beforehand). 
- Stir-fry shrimp 45 sec (toss for even cooking). When they turn white, turn off heat. 
6. Combine 
- Add 1 dash sesame oil and veggies, including liquid.
- Mix well and serve.
