# Soybean stew
Based off [Maangchi](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/kongbiji-jjigae)

## Ingredients
- 7 large dried anchovies
- 4 x 5 inch piece of dried kelp
- 4 cups water
- 1/2 cup dried soybeans (90 grams), washed and soaked in cold water for 12 hours
- 8 ounces pork stew meat, cut into bite sized pieces
- 1/2 cup chicken broth // if no chicken broth, substitute with kelp stock (see step 1 below)
- 1 teaspoon soy sauce
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 2 teaspoons toasted sesame oil
- 2 garlic cloves, minced 
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1 tablespoon saeujeot (salted fermented shrimp)
- 2 green onions, chopped

## Instructions
1. Make anchovy kelp stock. Put the anchovies and dried kelp in a sauce pan. Add the water and boil for 20 minutes over medium high heat. Strain the liquid into a large bowl and set aside.
2. Cook pork meat, 1.5 cups kelp stock, and chicken broth in Instant Pot for 28 minutes. Save remaining kelp stock for another use. 
3. Rinse the beans and drain. They will expand to a little more than 1 cup. Blend the beans with 1 cup water until creamy. (You'll need to blend a lot, maybe several minutes, to get it creamy.) You will get about 2 cups ground beans.
4. Heat a Dutch oven over medium high heat. Add the sesame oil, garlic, and onion. Stir with a wooden spoon and cook until onion is soft. 
5. Pour the ground beans and pork-stock mix into the Dutch oven. 
6. Gently stir and cook 5–6 minutes until the beans are fully cooked and bubbling, and some of the liquid evaporates. Taste it - the beans should taste nutty when fully cooked.
7. Add the salted fermented shrimp, green onion, and soy sauce. Stir and cook ~ 1 minute for the flavors to meld.
8. Serve with kimchi.
