# Spaghetti bolognese
Based on [Food 52](https://food52.com/recipes/26984-nigel-slater-s-really-good-spaghetti-bolognese)

## Ingredients
- 4 tablespoon butter
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 2 large cloves garic, diced
- 1 carrot, washed, peeled, and diced
- 2 stalks celery, washed, peeled, and diced
- 2 large, flat mushrooms such as portobello (about 4 ounces), finely chopped
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 pound ground beef or lamb
- 1 cup crushed tomatoes or passata
- 3/4 cup stock
- 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 3/4 cup cream

For serving
- 8 ounce Spaghetti or tagliatelle
- 1 handful grated Parmigiano Reggiano, to taste

## Instructions
1. In a large, heavy-based pot, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic, then cook until soft.
2. Add the carrot and celery, then cook until soft.
3. Add the mushrooms. Tuck in the bay leaves and cook for 10 minutes, stirring frequently.
4. Gently push the bay leaves to the side and add the meat, breaking it up well with a fork.
5. When the meat starts to brown, after about 3–4 minutes, stir and break up meat as necessary. Cook for another minute.
6. Mix in the tomatoes, stock, nutmeg, and black pepper.
7. Bring to a boil, then turn down heat so that there's just barely bubbling (not quite a simmer). Partially cover pot with a lid and cook for 1.5 hours, stirring occasionally and adding liquid as needed to avoid dryness.
8. Slowly pour in the cream, stir, and cook for 20 minutes.
9. Meanwhile cook the pasta, drain, and toss with olive oil to prevent sticking.
10. Remove the bay leaves from the bolognese pot. Toss in the pasta. 
11. Serve with grated Parmesan.
