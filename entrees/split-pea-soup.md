# Split pea soup

## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons olive oil 
- 4 large leeks, dark green tops removed, sliced into thin half moons (can sub onion if leek not available)
- 1 carrot, chopped small
- 1 large rib celery, chopped 
- 3 cloves garlic, peeled and minced
- 6 oz dried green split peas, picked over and rinsed
- 6 cups chicken stock/broth
- 1 teaspoon thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- For serving: cilantro, lemon zest and juice separated, red pepper flakes, EVOO, creme fraiche or mascarpone, bread for croutons

## Instructions
1. Place peas in enough hot water to cover them. Chop veggies (leeks, carrot, celery, garlic). 
2. Heat oil in Dutch oven over medium heat. Add veggies. Cook/stir occasionally until soft and beginning to brown at edges, ~ 10 min.
3. Add peas with water, broth, thyme, bay leaf. Stir.
4. Increase heat and bring to boil. Lower heat and low simmer until peas are very soft, ~ 60 min.
