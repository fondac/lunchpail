# Stuffed peppers
Based on [Love and Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/vegetarian-stuffed-peppers/#wprm-recipe-container-72831)

## Ingredients
- 4 red bell peppers, halved lengthwise, seeds and membranes removed
- 2 tablespoons olive oil, plus more for drizzling
- 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
- [omit] 1 jalapeño pepper, stemmed and finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- [omit] 2 teaspoons chili powder
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- [omit] 3/4 teaspoon sea salt
- ground black pepper
- 1/2 cup brown rice (or 1 1/2 cups cooked brown rice - cook ahead of time if possible)
- 2/3 cup black lentils, drained and rinsed (or 1 1/2 cups cooked lentils - cook ahead of time if possible) 
- 1/4 cup sliced almonds 
- 1 cup corn kernels, fresh or frozen
- 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice, plus wedges for serving
- 1 teaspoon lime zest
- 1 cup shredded pepper jack cheese // substitute monterey jack, cheddar, or mozzarella 

Serving sauce
- 1 bunch cilantro, bottom stems removed
- 1/2 yellow onion, roughly chopped
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1/2 cup yogurt

## Instructions
1. Cook brown rice. Cook lentils until very soft. 
2. Preheat the oven to 450 F. Place the peppers, cut side up, on metal baking sheet. Drizzle with olive oil and bake for 10 minutes, or until tender. Tip out and discard any liquid that pools inside the peppers. Set the peppers aside while you prepare the filling.
3. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion, jalapeño, and garlic and cook, stirring occasionally, for 5 minutes, or until softened.
4. Add the tomato paste, chili powder, cumin, salt, and pepper and cook for 3 minutes, or until the tomato paste darkens and the spices are fragrant.
5. Remove from the heat and stir in the brown rice, lentils, almonds, corn, and lime juice and zest.
6. Fill the peppers with the rice mixture and top with the cheese. Place in a large baking dish and broil for 5-8 minutes, or until the cheese is melted.
7. Mix ingredients for serving sauce in food processor until creamy. Store in airtight container until ready to serve peppers.
