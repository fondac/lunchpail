# Turkey chili with cheesy cornbread dumplings
Adapted from [Tasty](https://acrobat.adobe.com/link/track?uri=urn%3Aaaid%3Ascds%3AUS%3Aa2c6e291-0ce2-4112-8263-0e4fb19bc6fd&viewer%21megaVerb=group-discover)

## Ingredients
Chili
- 1 pound ground turkey
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 2 bell peppers, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 28-ounce can of crushed tomatoes, with juices
- 1 15-ounce can of black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 15-ounce can of kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup frozen corn, thawed
- [omit] 2 teaspoons chili powder
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper

Dumplings
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup whole milk
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 cup fine yellow cornmeal
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese

To serve
- 3 stalks green onions, sliced
- [omit] 1 jalapeño, sliced
- 1/2 cup cilantro, chopped

## Instructions
1. Add all the chili ingredients to the Instant Pot. Cook on high pressure for 25 minutes. Allow for natural release.
2. Meanwhile, make the dumpling batter. Combine the butter, eggs, and milk in a large bowl. Add the flour, cornmeal, and baking powder. Stir until smooth, then fold in the cheese.
3. When the Instant Pot is safe to open, use a small scoop to dollop the dumpilng batter on top of the chili. Cook on high pressure for 6 minutes. Check that the dumplings are puffed, tender, and cooked through.
4. Cook with any desired garnishes.
