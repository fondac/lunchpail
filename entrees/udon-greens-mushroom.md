# Udon with greens and mushroom

## Ingredients
- 10 oz dried udon noodles
- 5 tablespoons avocado oil
- 1 pound fresh shiitake mushrooms
- 1 bunch collard greens, washed, stemmed, cut lengthwise then into 1/4-inch strips
- 3 tablespoons black vinegar
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 3 tablespoons toasted sesame oil
- 2 tablespoons sesame seeds

## Instructions
1. In a large pot, boil 4 quarts water. Add noodles and cook, stirring, until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of the cooking water. Drain, rinse with cold water, then drain again. Toss with 1 tablespoon of oil and set aside. 
2. In a large stainless steel skillet, heat 2 tablespoons oil over medium and add mushrooms. Cook, stirring occasionally, until mushrooms are soft. Set aside mushrooms.
3. Add 1–2 tablespoons oil to skillet if needed. Sauté collard greens until wilted and soft, maybe 5 minutes depending on cookware.
4. Add udon, mushrooms, and 1/4 cup of the reserved cooking water. Cook, tossing, until noodles are coked through. Add more water if noodles appear dry.
5. Remove skillet from heat. Toos noodles with vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, and sesame seeds.
