# Udon with greens and mushroom

## Ingredients
- 10 oz dried udon noodles
- 5 tablespoons avocado oil
- 1 package boneless pork tenderloin or chop, cut into large bite-size strips (optional) // include this 
- 1 pound fresh shiitake mushrooms, stems removed and stored separately
- 1 bunch collard greens, stemmed, cut lengthwise then into 1/4-inch strips
- 3 tablespoons black vinegar
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 3 tablespoons toasted sesame oil
- 2 tablespoons sesame seeds

## Instructions
1. In a large pot, boil 4 quarts water. Add noodles and cook, stirring, until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of the cooking water. Drain, rinse with cold water, then drain again. Toss with 1 tablespoon of oil and set aside. 
2. In a large stainless steel skillet, heat 2 tablespoons oil over medium. Add pork and stir occasionally until cooked through. Set aside pork. 
3. Heat 1–2 more tablespoons oil in skillet if needed. Add mushrooms. Cook, stirring occasionally, until mushrooms are soft. Set aside mushrooms.
4. Add 1–2 tablespoons oil to skillet if needed. Sauté collard greens until wilted and soft, maybe 5 minutes depending on cookware.
5. Add udon, pork, mushrooms, and 1/4 cup of the reserved cooking water. Cook, tossing, until noodles are cooked through. Add more water if noodles appear dry.
6. Remove skillet from heat. Toos noodles with vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, and sesame seeds.
