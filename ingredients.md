Protein
- animal: eggs, poultry, seafood (like salmon sardines trout), and red meat
- plant: beans, peas, lentils, legumes, nuts, seeds, and soy

Fermented foods
- sauerkraut, kimchi, kefir, miso

Servings per day: 3 full-fat dairy, 3 veg, 2 fruit, 2–4 whole grains

Healthy fats: nuts, seeds, olives, avocado, olive oil, butter, beef tallow

Sodium recommendations // cross-check with iodine
- Ages 1–3: less than 1,200 mg per day
- Ages 4–8: less than 1,500 mg per day
- Ages 9–13: less than 1,800 mg per day

It may take 8–10 exposures before a young child is willing to try a new food. 

Protein, calcium, vitamin D, iron, folate, healthy fats
- pregnant women also need iodine, choline, omega-3s
- lactating women also need B12, omega-3s, folate, vitamin A
- Vegetarian diets often fall short in vitamins D and E, choline, and iron
- Vegan diets show broader shortfalls in vitamins A, D, E, B6, and B12; iodine; riboflavin; niacin; choline; calcium; iron; magnesium; phosphorus; potassium; zinc; and protein.
