# Saucy onion chicken
Adapted from [The Mediterranean Dish](https://www.themediterraneandish.com/baked-chicken-thighs/#wprm-recipe-container-38986)

## Ingredients
- 8 bone-in chicken thighs, trimmed of excess fat
- Kosher salt
- 5 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1/3 cup olive oil, more as needed
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried parsley
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 3 medium yellow onions, halved and thinly sliced
- 1 vine ripe tomato, halved and sliced
- 1 bag frozen okra 
- 1/4 cup water

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425 F. Sprinkle the chicken thighs with kosher salt on both sides and underneath the skins. Set aside.
2. Wet rub: In a small bowl or measuring cup, combine the tomato paste, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, parsley, paprika, cumin, and black pepper. Whisk until everything is well incorporated.
3. Coat the chicken with the wet rub on both sides, including underneath the skins.
4. In a glass 9" X 13" baking dish, place 3/4 of the sliced onions. Layer the chicken on top of the onions. Tuck the remaining onion and tomato between the chicken thighs. Add as much okra as will fit. 
5. Pour the water into the corners of the baking dish, taking care not to disturb the rub on the chicken pieces.
6. Bake until chicken is cooked through, about 40 minutes. Stir a couple times while cooking to prevent edges from burning.
