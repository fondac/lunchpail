# Baked chicken thighs

Based on NYTimes "Poulet Provencal" and [baked chicken thighs](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1024625-baked-chicken-thighs) 

## Ingredients
- 4 chicken legs or 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs, patted dry
- 1/3 cup avocado oil
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoons herbes de Provence
- 1 lemon, sliced

## Instructions
1. // Try this recipe with chicken pre-refrigerated for 8 hours or overnight to dry skin like in recipe for baked chicken with lemon. 
2. In a large bowl, combine the oil, mustard, and spices. Zest the lemon over the bowl, reserving the rest of the lemon for serving. Whisk until combined.
3. Add the chicken thighs to the bowl, tossing until fully coated in the marinade. Cover and let marinate for 30 minutes at room temperature, or up to 12 hours in the refrigerator.
4. Heat the oven to 425 degrees and lift the chicken thighs from the marinade, letting any excess drip into the bowl. Place the thighs on a sheet pan and let them sit at room temperature while the oven heats.
5. Bake until fully cooked and lightly browned, 25 to 30 minutes. An internal thermometer inserted into the thickest part of the meat should read 165 to 175 degrees.
6. Broil for a minute or two to crisp up skin // can't remember if I tried this
7. Serve with lemon slices.
