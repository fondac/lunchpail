# Baked tofu

From [It Doesn't Taste Like Chicken](https://itdoesnttastelikechicken.com/baked-tofu-bites/#recipe)

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup nutritional yeast
- 2 tablespoons avocado oil 
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce, (gluten-free if preferred)
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 2 blocks (350g each) firm (not extra firm) tofu, drained 

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375 F
2. In a large bowl, mix together the nutritional yeast, olive oil, soy sauce and garlic powder.
3. Break the block of tofu into small chunks and add them to the bowl. (No need to press the tofu first). Toss the tofu well to evenly coat in the sauce mixture.
4. Spread the tofu evenly across the baking sheets and bake for about 30 minutes, stirring halfway through, until lightly golden.
