# Chicken parmesan
Based on [AllRecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/223042/chicken-parmesan/)

## Ingredients
- 4 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
- Ground black pepper
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup panko bread crumbs, or more as needed
- 3/4 cup freshly grated Parm. Reg. cheese, divided
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour, or more if needed
- 1/2 cup avocado oil for frying, or more as needed
- 1/2 cup tomato sauce
- 1/4 cup fresh mozzarella, cut into small cubes
- 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
- 1/2 cup fresh provolone cheese, grated
- 2 teaspoons avocado oil

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 425 F.
2. Cut a freezer bag into 2 sheets and place chicken breasts between them on a solid, level surface. Firmly pound chicken with the smooth side of a meat mallet to a thickness of 1/2-inch. Let rest 10–15 minutes, then pat dry with paper towels. 
3. Season chicken with black pepper. Using a sifter or strainer, sprinkle flour over chicken breasts, evenly coating both sides.
4. Beat eggs in a shallow bowl and set aside. Mix bread crumbs and 1/2 cup Parm. Reg. cheese in a separate bowl. Dip a flour-coated chicken breast in the eggs, then let excess drip and transfer breast to the bread crumb mixture. Press crumbs into both sides. Place in glass baking dish. Repeat for each breast. 
5. Bake chicken for 15–20 minutes until golden and crispy. Do not brown the chicken; you'll cook it more in the oven later.
6. Top each breast with 2 tablespoons tomato sauce. Layer each chicken breast with equal amounts of mozzarella cheese, fresh basil, and provolone cheese. Sprinkle remaining Parmesan over top and drizzle each with 1/2 teaspoon avocado oil.
7. Bake until cheese has melted and chicken breasts are no longer pink in the center and center is at least 165 F, about 5 minutes. Do not brown cheese if this will be reheated later.
