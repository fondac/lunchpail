# Meatballs

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground ground turkey 
- 1/2 c Panko breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/4 c yogurt
- 1/2 teaspoon coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon sweet paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon turmeric
- 2 tablespoons dried parsley
- 1 tablespoon avocado oil

## Instructions
1. Get out large bowl. Add meat, breadcrumbs, egg, garlic, yogurt, spices, herbs. Mix.
2. Light spread of avocado oil on baking sheet pan. Add meatballs, ~ 1.5 inches in diameter.
3. Bake 10–12 min or until cooked through. (12 min on aluminum was perfect last time.) [Fonda to try glass dish next time?]
