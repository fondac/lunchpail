# Nut-crusted chicken cutlets
Based off ATK and Kenji

## Ingredients
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (6 to 8 ounces each), trimmed of excess fat and tenderloin removed
- 1 cup roughly chopped almonds, pulsed in food processor until they resemble coarse meal 
- 4 tablespoons (1/2 stick) unsalted butter, cut into 4 pieces
- 1 medium shallot, minced (about 3 tablespoons)
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt or 1/4 teaspoon table salt
- 1 cup panko (Japanese-style bread crumbs)
- 2 teaspoons finely grated zest from 1 lemon, zested lemon cut into 4 wedges
- 1 teaspoon minced fresh thyme leaves
- 3 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
- 1/2 cup unbleached all-purpose flour
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 3/4 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- [omit] 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper 

## Instructions
1. Adjust oven rack to lower-middle position and heat oven to 350 degrees. Set a wire rack on a rimmed baking sheet, then place parchment paper over the rack.
2. Using fork, poke thickest half of each breast 5 to 6 times. Place on parchment-topped rack and evenly sprinkle each breast with salt. Refrigerate, uncovered, while preparing coating.
3. Heat butter in 12-inch skillet over medium heat. Cook, swirling pan constantly, until butter turns golden and has nutty aroma, 4 to 5 minutes. Add shallot and salt. Cook until golden, stirring constantly, about 2 minutes.
4. Reduce heat to medium-low, add panko and ground nuts. Cook until, stirring frequently, 10–12 minutes. Transfer panko mixture to shallow dish or pie plate, then stir in lemon zest and thyme.
5. Lightly beat eggs and mustard together in second shallow dish or pie plate.
6. In third shallow dish or pie plate, mix flour with black pepper, paprika, garlic powder, and cayenne.
7. Pat chicken dry with paper towels. If parchment is wet, discard and/or replace. 
8. One at a time, dredge each chicken breast in flour and shake off excess, then coat with egg mixture and allow excess to drip off, then coat all sides with panko mixture and press gently so that crumbs adhere. Place chicken on rack. 
9. Bake until instant-read thermometer inserted into thickest part of chicken registers 160 degrees, 20–25 minutes. Let rest 5 minutes before serving with lemon wedges.
