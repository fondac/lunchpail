# Pseudo chicken cacciatore
Based on [NYTimes](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1015416-chicken-cacciatore)

## Ingredients
- 2 packages boneless chicken thighs, trimmed of excess fat
- kosher salt for sprinkling
- ground black pepper for sprinkling
- 5 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1/3 cup avocado oil
- 2 tablespoon dried parsley
- 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
- 1 onion, minced
- 2 carrots, minced
- 2 ribs celery, minced
- 6 cloves garlic, whole
- 1 pound mushrooms, cleaned, trimmed, cut in half, and sliced 
- 1 28-ounce can chopped tomatoes, including juice

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425 F. Sprinkle the chicken thighs with kosher salt and black pepper on both sides and underneath the skins. Set aside.
2. Wet rub: In a small bowl or measuring cup, combine the tomato paste, oil, parsley, and rosemary. Whisk until everything is well incorporated.
3. Coat the chicken with the wet rub on both sides, including underneath the skins.
4. In a glass 9" X 13" baking dish (may need 2), place about half of the vegetables. Layer the chicken on top.
5. Add the remaining vegetables between the chicken thighs. Try not to disturb the rub on the chicken pieces. 
6. Bake until chicken is cooked through, about 40 minutes. Stir a couple times while cooking to prevent edges from burning.
