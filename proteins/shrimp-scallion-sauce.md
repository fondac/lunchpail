# Sauteed shrimp with scallion sauce
Based on Milk Street's mo hanh adaptation in May–June 2020 print issue

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon avocado oil
- 1.5 pounds extra large shrimp, peeled and deveined

Scallion sauce
- 1 bunch scallions, chopped finely
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 cup avocado oil
- 1.5 tablespoons fish sauce
- 1.5 tablespoons finely grated ginger

## Instructions
Sauce
1. In a medium heatproof bowl, combine the scallions and pepper. Using your fingers, gently rub the salt and pepper into the scallions until they begin to wilt.
2. In a small saucepan over medium high, heat the oil until shimmering. Pour the hot oil over the scallions. Stir to combine.
3. Stir in the fish sauce and ginger. Cool to room temperature.

Shrimp
1. In a large skillet or wok, heat oil over high until shimmering. Add shrimp in an even layer.
2. Stir occasionally until the shrimp are opaque throughout. Remove from heat. 
3. In storage or serving container, top with scallion sauce.
