# Roasted parsnips and beets

Parsnips
1. Wash, trim, peel, and cut into quarters lengthwise.
2. Toss in avocado oil, black pepper, and a pinch of salt.
3. Bake at 400 F for 20–30 minutes or until fork-tender.

Beets
1. Scrub and wrap loosely in foil.
2. Bake at 400 F for 2–2.5 hours or until fork-tender.
