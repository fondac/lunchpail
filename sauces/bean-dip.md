# Bean dip

## Beans
Put 1/3 cup beans in Instant Pot. Cover by at least 1 inch of water. Cook on high pressure for ~ 40 minutes. 
- Black beans: 20 to 25 minutes
- Black-eyed peas : 20 to 25 minutes
- Great Northern beans: 25 to 30 minutes
- Navy beans: 25 to 30 minutes
- Pinto beans: 25 to 30 minutes
- Cannellini beans: 35 to 40 minutes
- Chickpeas (garbanzo beans) : 35 to 40 minutes
- Red kidney beans: 25-30 minutes (boil for 10 minutes before pressure cooking)

## Dip
Drain beans and put in food processor. Add: 
- 1–2 tbsp lemon juice 
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil 
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder 
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro or parsley 
- 3/4 cup (6 oz) yogurt — try adding this last, a little at a time because I’m not sure this is the right quantity

Cut vegetables (e.g., 1 carrot and 1 celery stalk into batons) for serving.
