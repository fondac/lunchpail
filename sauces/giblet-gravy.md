# Giblet gravy

Based on [The Pioneer Woman](https://www.thepioneerwoman.com/food-cooking/recipes/a11306/giblet-gravy/)

## Ingredients
- Giblets and neck, saved from the uncooked chicken
- Drippings from roasted chicken, fat separated // try subbing 1/4 cup duck fat
- 1/2 c. all-purpose flour (more if needed)
- 4 c. no-sodium chicken, chicken, or vegetable broth (more if needed)
- Salt and pepper

*** try adding parsley next time, or mint, or dried orange / apple peel

## Instructions
1. Take the giblets and neck from the raw chicken and cover them with water by 2 inches in a small saucepan.
2. Bring to a gentle boil over medium heat, then reduce the heat to low and simmer it for 1 hour to both to cook the meat and to make a giblet broth for the gravy.
3. Remove the giblets and neck from the water (don’t worry; they’re supposed to look really grody) and set them aside. Store the broth in in a large bowl for later.
4. Add 1 cup of the fat and 1/2 c of flour to the saucepan over medium heat. Whisk to make a roux/paste that's stirrable and not overly greasy. Add more fat or flour as needed. Keep whisking and allow to cook to deep golden brown color.
5. Pour in 1 cup of the drippings (if available) and the chicken broth, whisking constantly for 5-8 minutes as it thickens.
6. Use your fingers to remove as much of the neck meat as you can and chop up the giblets into fine pieces. Add enough meat to the gravy to make it chunky.
7. If gravy seems too thick, add more broth. 
8. Add parsley, black pepper, and salt to taste.
