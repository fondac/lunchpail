# Pasta sauce

## Ingredients
- 1.5 tablespoons avocado oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tablespoons tomato paste, if measured (or 2 of the heaping ones from the freezer)
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/4 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 28-oz can peeled tomatoes, juices included
- [omit] heavy cream

## Instructions
1. Heat the oil in a stainless steel skillet over medium-low heat. Sauté the onion until soft and translucent. Add the garlic and cook until softened and fragrant.
3. Add the tomato paste, oregano, thyme, and black pepper. Cook until tomato paste color deepens, mixing well and stirring frequently.
4. Add the tomatoes and juices, either squeezing the tomatoes into smaller pieces by hand before they go in the skillet (crushing in the can helps prevent splashing) or breaking them up with a large spoon after pouring the can into the skillet.
5. Turn up the heat to medium. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the sauce boils gently for a minute or two.
6. [Omit] Stir in heavy cream, turn heat down to low, and cook until just combined.
