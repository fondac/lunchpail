# Baked plantains

## Ingredients
- 2–4 plantains
- 1 tbsp salt
- 3 cups water
- avocado oil

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425 F. Cut through plantain peel lengthwise. Cut inside fruit into slices 1” or thinner.
2. Add salt and water to a large bowl. Soak plantain slices for 15 min. 
3. Pat slices dry and place in another large bowl. Add enough oil to coat slices when tossed.
4. Place on aluminum sheet pan (no parchment or mat). Bake for 15 min.
5. Let slices cool slightly. Smash each slice by placing between 2 small pieces of parchment paper, then pressing on top parchment piece with bottom of large ceramic mug (best to use one with a flat bottom).
6. Place slices back on sheet pan. Bake another 15 min.
