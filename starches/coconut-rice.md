# Coconut rice

## Ingredients
- 2 cups brown rice, rinsed 3 times
- 2 (14-ounce) cans of full-fat coconut milk
- 2 tablespoons dried cilantro
- Juice and zest of 1 lime

## Instructions
1. Combine the rice, coconut milk, and cilantro in a pot and bring to a boil. Cover, reduce the heat, and simmer undisturbed for 45 minutes.
2. Remove from heat and let sit, covered, for 10 minutes.
3. Fluff with a fork. Mix in lime juice and zest.
