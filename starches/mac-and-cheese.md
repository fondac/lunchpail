# Mac and cheese
Adapted from [Once upon a chef](https://www.onceuponachef.com/recipes/mac-and-cheese.html#recipe), [Tasty](https://acrobat.adobe.com/link/track?uri=urn%3Aaaid%3Ascds%3AUS%3Ab919b42e-ff5e-4760-bc56-0984cad57252&viewer%21megaVerb=group-discover)

## Ingredients
- 1 pound macaroni, cavatappi, or penne pasta
- 1 stick unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 5 cups milk
- 4 cups Gruyere cheese, shredded // 16 oz block should be more than enough
- 2 cups sharp cheddar, shredded // 8 oz block should be enough, do not use extra sharp
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 3/4 cup Parmigiano Reggiano, grated
- 1 cup Panko breadcrumbs
- 1 teaspoon herbes de Provence or thyme

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375 F. Boil pasta until al dente. Meanwhile, shred and grate the cheeses separately. 
2. Drain pasta and rinse with cold water.
3. In a medium pot, melt 6 tablespoons of the butter over low heat. Add flour and whisk for 2 minutes or until completely smooth.
4. Slowly add milk and whisk for 4 minutes or until sauce has thickened.
5. Remove pot from heat and whisk in the gruyere, cheddar, black pepper, and nutmeg. If necessary to fully melt cheese, place pot back over low heat, but do not let sauce boil.
6. Add the pasta and stir to coat it in the sauce.
7. Pour the sauced pasta into a 9x13-in glass baking dish.
8. In a small bowl, mix the Parmigiano Regianno, breadcrumbs, and herbes de Provence or thyme. Sprinkle mixture over the baking dish.
9. Bake for 30 minutes or until golden.
