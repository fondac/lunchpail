## Turkey
Adapt from Smitten Kitchen https://smittenkitchen.com/2019/11/dry-brined-turkey-with-roasted-onions/
but skip the maple syrup and chile, and try roasting turkey upside down first for 25 min then turn over // save recipe  

Ingredients
- 10-pound heirloom turkey
- Kosher salt (new box)
- Lots of unsalted butter, try 1 stick, melted 
- 3 yellow onions, 3 red onions, peeled and quartered
- Herb(s) like rosemary for stuffing 

Instructions
1. Brining … 
2. Take out of fridge. Come to room temp 2 hrs. Discard any juices that have collected around the pan. // After 1 h 15 min … 
3. Preheat oven to 500 with baking stone for 45 min. 
4. Discard any juices that have collected around pan. Combine butter, 1 tbsp high heat oil, parsley, garlic. Rub between skin and meat, and a little outside skin. Put herbs in cavity. 
5. Toss onions in oil. Arrange around turkey so they prop up as much of the turkey legs as possible for air circulation. 
6. Oven to 400. Put turkey in pan directly on baking stone on lowest level. 
7. Roast for 20-45 min or until golden brown. 
8. Reduce temp to 300. Periodically baste with 1-2 tbsp of butter then turkey juice. Rotate pan a couple times. Loosely tent with foil if skin gets too dark. 
9. Time depends on size but after 1.5 hrs might be ready. Turn off oven when breast temp is 158. 
10. Loosely tent with foil (if haven’t already) and let sit for 45 min. Check breast temp maybe halfway through - should be 165. 
11. Remove from oven and let sit another hour. 
12. Lots of juices may spill out when carving.
13. Save leftovers for soup
