# Chinese eggplant

## Ingredients
- 2–3 long Japanese or Chinese eggplant, sliced in half lengthwise and chopped on bias
- 2 teaspoons cornstarch, more if needed
- 2 tablespoons avocado oil
- 4 large cloves garlic, peeled and minced
- 1 tablespoon ginger, peeled and minced
- [omit] 2–3 Chinese dried red chilis, chopped
- 4 scallions, finely chopped on the bias

Sauce
- 1 teaspoon ground Szechuan or black peppercorns — consider a satchet for these
- 1 teaspoon chili flakes
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1 tablespoon chinkiang (black rice) vinegar
- 1 tablespoon shaoxing wine or dry sherry // sub with 1/2 tablespoon rice wine vinegar
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1/2 teaspoon five spice

## Instructions
1. Salt eggplant pieces generously and let them sit for 20 minutes to pull out extra water and bitterness.
2. Meanwhile, whisk ingredients for sauce in a small bowl and set aside. 
3. Rinse salt off eggplant, then pat eggplant dry with paper towels.
4. Place eggplant in a medium bowl and add cornstarch a little at a time. Toss well until every piece has an even white coating. Shake off any loose powder.
5. In a large stainless steel skillet over medium heat, add 1 tablespoon avocado oil and eggplant. Stir fry until the sides are golden and the center is mushy-tender.
6. Transfer eggplant to a plate and add remaining 1 tablespoon avocado oil to the pan. Add garlic, ginger, and red chilis, then cook for 1 minute until fragrant and softened.
7. Add eggplant and scallions, toss, and pour in sauce. Stir fry for 1 minute, until all the vegetables are coated.
8. Serve with rice. Allow people to self-serve sauce.
