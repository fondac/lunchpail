# Roasted vegetables

## Beets
1. Scrub and wrap loosely in foil.
2. Bake at 400 F for 2–2.5 hours or until fork-tender.

## Bell peppers
1. Wash and slice. Toss in avocado oil.
2. Roast at 375 F on metal sheet pan (no parchment) until soft. 

## Cauliflower
1. Toss with garlic powder, paprika, and black pepper.
2. Bake cauliflower at 375 F for 30–45 minutes or until very soft (not al dente)

## Cauliflower and radishes
1. Scrub radishes and cut off ends.
2. Slice radishes and cut cauliflower into small bite-size pieces. Toss both in avocado oil and 1/2 teaspoon black pepper.
3. Roast at 375 F on metal sheet pan (no parchment) until soft. 

## Green beans
1. Toss in avocado oil and 1/2 teaspoon black pepper.
2. Roast at 375 F on metal sheet pan (no parchment) until soft. 

## Japanese sweet potato
1. Wash and scrub clean. Pierce with fork 8 times all around the sweet potato.
2. Bake at 375 for 60–90 minutes until very soft when pierced with fork.

## Parsnips
1. Wash, trim, peel, and cut into quarters lengthwise.
2. Toss in avocado oil, black pepper, and a pinch of salt.
3. Bake on metal sheet pan at 400 F for 20–30 minutes or until fork-tender.
